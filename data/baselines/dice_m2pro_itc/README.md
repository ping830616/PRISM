# Frozen DICE Result Summaries

These compact files are copied from the DICE Apple M2 Pro result directories at
the exact revision recorded in
`../../external/dice_m2pro_itc/source.json`.

- `mixed/`: DICE mixed-profile headline and holdout summaries.
- `full/`: DICE full-profile headline and holdout summaries.

They are reference outputs for clean-environment reproduction and regression
checks. They are not PRISM results and must be labeled as DICE/ITC baseline
evidence in figures and tables.
